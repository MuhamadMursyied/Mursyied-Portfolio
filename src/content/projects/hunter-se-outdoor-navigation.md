## Overview

At MIMOS Berhad I manage the navigation system of the **Hunter SE** robot so it can operate autonomously outdoors.

## What I did

- Combined **GPS, motion sensors (IMU) and wheel data** with an **EKF** for accurate positioning.
- Managed the robot's **Nav2** navigation system.
- Took each change from **Gazebo** simulation to field deployment.
- Runs on an **NVIDIA Jetson**.

**Stack:** ROS 2, Nav2, EKF, GNSS, Gazebo, NVIDIA Jetson
