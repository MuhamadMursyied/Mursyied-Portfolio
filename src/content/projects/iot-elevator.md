## Overview

- Simulated **two elevators with four floors each**.
- Used a **three-tier architecture**: presentation, logic and data.
- Ran **four Qt projects on separate PCs** that talk to each other over **Wi-Fi**.
- Coded in **C++** with **Qt Creator** and an **Arduino UNO**.
