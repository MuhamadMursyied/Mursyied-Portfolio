## Overview

The original **Timmy** UGV used an integrated PCB, which made replacing components difficult. I am redesigning it as a modular platform.

## Objectives

- A **modular architecture** so hardware can be replaced easily.
- A **hands-on platform** for learning and testing.
- **Flexible integration** of sensors, controllers and communication modules.
